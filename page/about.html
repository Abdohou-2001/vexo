Our Story

Built for Professional HVAC Service

VEXO HVAC was created to deliver HVAC service the way homeowners expect it should be — professional, transparent, and respectful of their time and home. We understand how disruptive a no-cool or no-heat situation can be, especially in Texas climate.

Our approach is straightforward: listen to the customer, inspect the system thoroughly, explain what we find in clear language, and present practical options. No pressure tactics, no confusing jargon. You decide what works best for your home and budget.

From AC repair and installation to heating service, maintenance, and indoor air quality improvements, we handle each job with attention to detail — from proper setup and testing to cleanup and customer guidance. Our goal is reliable comfort and long-term performance, not just a quick fix.

"We treat every home like our own — professional work, clear communication, and results you can feel."

— VEXO HVAC Team
